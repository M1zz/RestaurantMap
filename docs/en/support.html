Support

Stuck on something? Take a look at the frequently asked questions below first.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add a restaurant?

  • Type the restaurant’s name into the search bar at the top of the map, then tap “Add to My Restaurants” in the results.
  • You can also tap a nearby place shown on the map to add it right away.
  • If a place doesn’t show up in search, add it yourself at your current location or at a spot you pick on the map.

What are Visited, Want to Go and Top 6?

  • Visited — restaurants you’ve already been to. You can log your visits to them.
  • Want to Go — restaurants you’d like to try someday. After you’ve been, switch it to Visited on the restaurant’s detail screen.
  • Top 6 (My Michelin) — rank your six favorite restaurants from 1st to 6th. Restaurants marked Want to Go can’t be added to your Top 6.

Do I have to rate the taste?

No, it’s optional. But rating flavor intensity and how much you liked it fills in your Taste Profile and makes it easy to look back on what each restaurant tasted like.

When does the “How was your visit today?” notification arrive?

If you stay near a saved restaurant for more than 5 minutes and then leave, the notification arrives about 40 minutes later. This feature needs location access set to “Always” and notifications turned on.

  • If you’d rather not get these notifications, go to Settings > Apps > Insaeng Matjip and turn off notifications, or change location access to “While Using the App”.
  • Location checks happen only on your device. Your movements are never stored or sent anywhere.

Can I use the app without allowing location access?

Yes. You can pick a spot on the map yourself to add and log restaurants. Only showing your current location and the after-visit notifications won’t be available.

Place search isn’t showing any results

  • Place search needs an internet connection.
  • Try searching again with a different name or a shorter keyword.
  • If it still doesn’t appear, you can pick a spot on the map and add it yourself.

Where is my log stored? Does it sync with my other devices?

Your restaurants, visit log and photos are all stored only on this iPhone. The current version doesn’t sync with other devices, and the iCloud backup and restore option in Settings doesn’t work yet. A full iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) does include the app’s data.

I want to erase everything

Open Settings from the map screen and tap “Delete All Data” to erase your restaurants and visit log. This can’t be undone, so please be careful. To remove your photos too, delete the app.

If I delete and reinstall the app, will my log still be there?

No. Because your log is stored only on your device, it’s erased along with the app. Please keep this in mind before deleting the app.
