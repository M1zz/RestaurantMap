Never forget a great meal again

Insaeng Matjip keeps the restaurants you’ve been to, and the ones you’re dying to try, together on one map. Log the dishes, photos, star ratings and taste notes from every visit, and the more you log, the clearer your own food map and your taste become.

Features

Find it on the map, save it in a tap

Search for a restaurant on the map, or tap a nearby place to add it straight to your list. You can also add one yourself at your current location or any spot you pick on the map.

Visited and Want to Go

Keep the places you’ve already been separate from the ones you’d like to try someday. Once you’ve been, just move it to Visited.

Visit log

Record the date, what you ate, photos, a star rating and notes. Save a restaurant’s dishes once, and next time you can simply pick them from the list.

Detailed taste ratings

Rate how strong each flavor was, like spiciness, saltiness and sweetness, and separately how much you liked it. Steak, sushi and ramen get their own criteria, such as doneness, rice and broth.

My Michelin Top 6

Rank your six favorite restaurants and keep them in a list of their own. Next time someone asks for a recommendation, it’s ready to show.

Taste Profile

Based on your taste ratings, see at a glance which flavors and styles of food you love most.

After-visit reminders

When you leave a restaurant you’ve saved, a gentle “How was your visit today?” notification arrives a little later so you don’t forget to log it. You can turn it off anytime.

Directions and calls

Open any saved restaurant in Apple Maps, KakaoMap or NAVER Map, and tap its phone number to call right away.

Your log stays on your iPhone

There’s no sign-up. Your restaurant list, visit log and photos are stored only on your iPhone. For details, please see our Privacy Policy.
